# Code of conduct

Evyro is used by developers in many countries, languages and experience levels. We want everyone to be able to ask questions, report problems and contribute without being made to feel unwelcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be patient with questions and with people writing in a second language.
- Give specific, actionable feedback, and accept it the same way.
- Respect privacy. Do not share other people's personal details or private logs.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## Where this applies

In the repository, its issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the repository (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and mark it as a conduct report, so only maintainers see it. Maintainers will review it, keep the reporter's details private, and may warn, edit or remove content, or restrict participation. Maintainers who are involved in a report step aside from deciding it.
